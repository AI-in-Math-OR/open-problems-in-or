Summary of Results

Aggregate outcomes of our agentic pipeline across every open problem extracted from the source journals/conferences. Figures are computed from the published dataset each time this page loads, so they always match the problem listings.

Read these figures as provisional. Write-ups are LLM-generated and passed an automated fidelity audit; a small subset of solutions are human-verified for testing, but not all of the generated solutions or partial progress. A problem counted as solved below has at least one solution write-up, not a verified proof.

At a glance

Progress across all problems

Each problem falls into exactly one bucket. “Partial progress” means no full write-up exists yet, but at least one partial result does.

What the solutions look like

Solution write-ups are classified by how the problem was resolved: an explicit construction achieving the goal, an exact characterization or dichotomy, a direct analytic estimate, a reduction or duality certificate, a class-wide impossibility or lower bound, or a counterexample refuting the claim.

How far the attempts got

The verifier stage rates every solver attempt out of three, against the problem's main result. Problems attempted more than once are counted at their best score.
