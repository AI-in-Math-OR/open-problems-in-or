Summary of Results

Aggregate outcomes of our agentic pipeline across every open problem extracted from the source journals. Figures are computed from the published dataset each time this page loads, so they always match the problem listings.

Read these figures as provisional. Write-ups are machine-generated and passed an automated fidelity audit; they are not independently refereed. A problem counted as solved below has at least one solution write-up, not a verified proof.

At a glance

Progress across all problems

Each problem falls into exactly one bucket. “Partial progress” means no full write-up exists yet, but at least one partial result does.

What the solutions look like

Solution write-ups are classified by how the problem was resolved — by proving the statement, or by refuting it with a counterexample.

How far the attempts got

The verifier stage rates every solver attempt out of three, against the problem's main result. Problems attempted more than once are counted at their best score.
